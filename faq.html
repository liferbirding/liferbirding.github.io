Frequently asked questions

01Is Lifer free?

Yes. The whole game is free on iOS and Android.

02How does Lifer identify birds?

Take a photo of a bird and Lifer identifies what you've found. The clearer the photo, the higher the confidence. Tap Listen and Lifer names the birds singing around you. Sound ID runs on your phone and works offline.

03What is a lifer?

A species you've seen for the first time. Every new species you capture in Lifer is a lifer and earns bonus points.

04Can I add photos I took before Lifer?

Yes. Upload them from your library and they go straight onto your life list.

05Can I play with friends?

Yes. Add friends, compare Aviaries and compete on the leaderboard.

06Where do I download it?

App Store and Google Play.
